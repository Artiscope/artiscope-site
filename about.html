About

We make
the introduction.

Artiscope is a contemporary art publisher. We find independent artists worth knowing about, and send you straight to them.

Every artist here keeps full control of their own work, their own prices, and their own customers. Artiscope never touches a sale, it just makes the introduction.

You buy directly from the artist, every time.

No middleman handling your payment or your data.

Shipping, framing, and returns are all handled by them, not us.
